## Why Local SEO is Different from Regular SEO

You don't need to compete with Times of India or Amazon. You need to appear when someone in Behala, Dum Dum, or Park Street searches for what you sell. That's a much smaller, more winnable game.

Local SEO in Kolkata means optimising for:
- "graphic designer near Park Street"
- "best bakery in Salt Lake"
- "website development company in Kolkata"

## The Three Things That Actually Move Rankings

### 1. Google Business Profile (Most Important)

If you do nothing else, complete your Google Business Profile. Businesses with complete, active profiles appear in the map pack - the 3 results at the top of local searches.

What "complete" means:
- Business name, address, phone (exact match to your website)
- Category set correctly ("South Indian Restaurant" beats "Restaurant")
- 10+ recent photos uploaded
- Responding to every review, including negative ones
- Posts added weekly (Google rewards active profiles)

### 2. On-Page Local Signals

Your website needs to say where you are and what you do - explicitly.
- Page title: "[Service] in Kolkata | [Business Name]"
- Your full address in text on the contact page
- An embedded Google Map on the contact page
- Your locality and neighbourhood mentioned in homepage copy

### 3. Consistent NAP

Your Name, Address, and Phone must be identical everywhere:
- Your website
- Google Business Profile
- JustDial and Sulekha listings
- Facebook page

Even small differences ("St." vs "Street") dilute your local authority.

## What Doesn't Work

- Buying backlinks from random sites
- Keyword stuffing in meta descriptions
- Cheap packages promising Page 1 in 30 days

## Realistic Timeline

- **Month 1-2**: Technical fixes, Google Business completion, on-page basics
- **Month 3-4**: Rankings move for less competitive terms
- **Month 5-6**: Consistent Page 1 for local terms
- **Month 6+**: Compounding growth as authority builds
