## The E-commerce Opportunity in West Bengal

West Bengal's e-commerce market is growing faster than national averages, driven by tier-2 and tier-3 adoption. For Kolkata-based businesses, this creates a window: local brands with a professional online presence can capture customers before the market saturates.

## Platform Comparison

### Shopify
**Best for**: Product-based businesses with 20+ SKUs wanting minimal technical work.
**Monthly cost**: ₹2,117-₹8,114/month.
**Watch out for**: No Shopify Payments in India - factor in Razorpay transaction fees.

### WooCommerce (WordPress)
**Best for**: Businesses wanting full control with lower recurring cost.
**Watch out for**: Requires active maintenance - hosting, plugin updates, security patches.

### Custom Next.js / React
**Best for**: Unique requirements, serious scale ambitions, or specific integrations.
**Best ROI when**: Your catalogue or checkout has requirements platforms can't handle.

## What Most Guides Miss: Logistics

Your website can be perfect and still fail if logistics aren't sorted. Before launch:
- Set up Shiprocket or Delhivery
- Define delivery zones and timelines clearly
- Write your return policy before your first sale, not after your first return

## The Minimum Viable E-commerce Store

Best performing new launches start with:
- 10-20 tightly curated products
- Clean product photos (natural light works)
- Working WhatsApp chat for pre-purchase questions
- One social platform updated consistently

## Getting Your First 50 Orders

The biggest mistake: building the store and waiting for Google traffic. Google takes time. For the first 3-6 months:
- Paid Meta/Instagram ads for awareness
- WhatsApp broadcasts to existing customers
- Google Shopping ads for specific product searches
- Opening discount for Kolkata first-time buyers
